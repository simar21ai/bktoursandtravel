Where people go

Popular trips from Delhi

Just a few of the places we drive to most often. Every trip can be changed to suit your dates and budget.

Shimla & Manali

5 to 6 days · By road

Mall Road, Kufri, Solang Valley and Atal Tunnel. The classic first trip to the mountains.

Jaipur, Agra & Delhi

3 to 5 days · Golden Triangle

Taj Mahal at sunrise, Amber Fort and the old bazaars of the Pink City.

Rishikesh & Haridwar

2 to 3 days · Weekend

Ganga aarti at Har Ki Pauri, cafes by the river, and rafting if you're up for it.

Char Dham Yatra

10 to 12 days · May to October

Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath with planned stops and rest days.

Amritsar

2 to 3 days · By road or train

Golden Temple, Wagah Border ceremony, and more food than you'll be able to finish.

Anywhere else in India

Goa, Kerala, Ladakh, the Northeast. If it's in India, ask us and we'll put a plan together.
